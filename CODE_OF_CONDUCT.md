# Code of Conduct

English · [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing experiences, viewpoints, and creative styles.
- Give specific, actionable feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and prioritize the community's overall interests.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, media, and other contributions that do not follow them. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report an incident privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed seriously, and the reporter's privacy will be protected to the extent reasonably possible.
