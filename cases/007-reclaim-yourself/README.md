The final 41.57-second vertical short follows an audio-first workflow: six independently generated Chinese voice segments, five 0.65-second breathing gaps, visuals cut to the accepted narration, 15 speech-linked captions, and music raised to 17% with automatic dialogue ducking. Its six-scene arc moves from a rain-soaked low point toward a mountain sunrise.
